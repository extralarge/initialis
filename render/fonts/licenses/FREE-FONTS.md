# Non-OFL font references

These fonts retain their own copyright and are not covered by the engine's MIT licence. The source downloads did not include a complete embedding/redistribution licence. The source descriptions below are retained as evidence; the MIT licence does not replace missing font-specific terms.

## Lighthaus

Copyright (c) 2012 Jerak Durzan. All rights reserved.

Downloaded unchanged from https://www.typedump.com/fonts/Lighthaus-Regular.otf ; extracted capital outlines are used in the renderer.

TypeDump describes the licence as **Free**: https://www.typedump.com/font/lighthaus . The font itself provides copyright and designer notices but no licence text. No primary author publication or explicit outline-redistribution terms were found in the checked sources. The catalogue's Free label is recorded without claiming an OFL or MIT licence.

## Saint

Design: **Liza Dushnota, Saint Agency**. Saint is retained in this fork with attribution.

Primary source: [Saint Free Display Typeface](https://www.behance.net/gallery/150260489/Saint-Free-Display-Typeface-Free-font-Typography), published by Saint Agency, Liza Dushnota and NASTASYA KOROLEVA on August 12, 2022. The authors describe Saint as a free display typeface and link to [their download folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ENL_f_sEJ98qvjOWyOKrWT0hVgXCwRrQ). The public folder contains `Saint Regular.ttf`; no separate licence document was visible when checked on November 1, 2026.

Uprock states free personal and commercial use with attribution to **Liza Dushnota, Saint Agency**: https://www.fonts.uprock.ru/fonts/saint . This is not an OFL font and is not covered by the project's MIT licence. The linked author's page confirms the free release; it does not explicitly describe redistribution of extracted glyph outlines.

## OFL sources

Google Fonts and Foglihten licence files are preserved alongside these notes. Dispater and Sinistre embed their OFL notices in the font name table; the extracted notices are included here. Lack Line is listed as OFL by TypeDump, with its source at https://gitlab.com/velvetyne/lack . Oi's OFL file was obtained from Google Fonts.
